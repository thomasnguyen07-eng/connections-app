Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Connections ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address and password (for account)
  • Name (optional)
  • Reports, descriptions, and images you upload
  • Contact information you provide in reports

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide our services
  • To moderate content for safety
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

Data Retention: We retain reports and images for a maximum of 180 days (6 months). After this period, data is automatically deleted from our servers. This policy helps us manage storage costs, protect user privacy, and ensure data remains current and relevant. All user data, including images, is permanently removed after 180 days.

3. Content Moderation

We use SightEngine to detect and block prohibited content including nudity, weapons, and violence.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage
  • SightEngine: Image moderation

5. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

To request account deletion:

  1. Email us at 📧 Support: support@connectionsfinder.com
  2. Include your account email address in the request
  3. We will process your request within 7 days

Upon deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account credentials
  • All reports you created
  • All images you uploaded
  • Your profile information

Some data may be retained for legal or fraud prevention purposes as required by law.

6. Your Rights

  • Delete your account and data anytime (see Section 5)
  • Contact us with questions
